`highest-and-best-use` legally-permissible test and the workup's zoning table.
All retrieved 2026-09-02. **These are snapshots — counties amend. For anything
load-bearing, confirm the current version against the source URL, and confirm
the subject's district with the county office. Never infer zoning from
surrounding use.**

## City ordinances — `cities\` subfolder

Counties are unincorporated-area rules only. **Cross-reference rule: determine
jurisdiction FIRST, every assignment.** The tracker's public-records block
carries the city-limits answer (Census place + TPAD city code); inside city
limits the CITY ordinance governs and the county PDF is the wrong document —
cities carry their own overlays, historic districts, and design standards the
county never mentions. All retrieved 2026-09-02.

**The pointer trap:** a municipal code's "Title 14 — Zoning" is sometimes one
page adopting a separate ordinance by reference (Tullahoma, Woodbury) and
sometimes the full ordinance (Manchester). Read the chapter before citing it.
