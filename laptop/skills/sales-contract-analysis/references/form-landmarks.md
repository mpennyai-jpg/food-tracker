# Form landmarks — where each fact sits, and the traps

Method only. No assignment data. Page numbers are the form's own footer
numbers ("Page 4 of 11"), not the PDF page — counter offers and cover
sheets shift the PDF count.

## Tennessee REALTORS RF401 — Purchase and Sale Agreement (version 01/01/2026, 11 pp)

| Form page | Section | What to pull |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | §1 Purchase and Sale | buyer(s), seller(s), address, county, deed book/page, instrument number, parcel ID; §1.A included items; **§1.B items that REMAIN** (personal property); §1.C items that shall NOT remain; §1.D leased items box; §1.E fuel |
| 1 | §2 Purchase Price | **price in numerals AND words** — both; §2.A loan up to __% of price |
| 2 | §2.A cont. | **loan type boxes**: Conventional / FHA / VA / USDA / Other (write-in — THDA appears here); loan obligations |
| 2 | §2.B | **Financing Contingency Waived** box — checked means cash/no financing contingency |
| 2–3 | §2.C Appraisal | **box 1 = NOT contingent on appraised value; box 2 = IS contingent** — read the pixels; box 2 gives the buyer 3 days to waive or terminate after a low appraisal |
| 3 | §2.D Closing Expenses | seller expenses, buyer expenses, **title expenses "shall be paid as follows: ___"**, the "may be modified as follows" lines, closing agencies for each side |
| 4 | §3 Earnest Money | amount, days after binding date, **holder** (title company or brokerage), check/wire; §3.A–B default and disbursement |
| 4 | §4.A Closing Date | **"the ___ day of ___, 20__"** — month names must be matched case-sensitively; the next line reads "or on such earlier date as **may** be agreed" and has turned into a closing month of May; possession boxes (at closing / temporary occupancy) |
| 4 | §4.B–C | prorations; **Greenbelt** boxes (15-acre minimum — usually neither applies) |
| 5 | §4.D–F, §5 | special assessments; warranties; association fees; §5 title and conveyance; **§5.C "Name(s) on Deed to be:" — the real buyer list**; §5.D lien payoff |
| 5 | §6 | public water / sewer availability clause |
| 5 | §7 Lead-Based Paint | **"does apply (Property built prior to 1978)" box** — feeds FHA peeling-paint MPR |
| 5–7 | §8 Inspections | buyer's right to inspect, days; **§8.D Resolution Period** (days); §8.E waiver of all inspections box |
| 7 | §9–11 | completion of repairs; final inspection; survey/flood, insurability, water supply, waste disposal |
| 8 | §12–15 | title exceptions; disclaimer; brokerage (compensation not set by law — relevant to a seller-paid buyer-agent commission stipulation); default; **§15 Home Protection Plan** waived / ordered |
| 9 | §16–17 | non-assignability; **§17.A Binding Agreement Date definition** (date of receipt of notice of acceptance); terminology — performance deadlines fall on business days |
| 10 | §18–20 | seller's additional obligations (disclosures); method of execution; exhibits and addenda |
| 10 | **§21 Special Stipulations** | **where the money hides**: seller-paid closing costs, buyer-agent commission, repair credits, appliances, occupancy terms. Always read this page |
| 10 | §22 Time Limit of Offer | |
| 11 | Signatures | buyers sign with offer date; **seller checks ACCEPTS / COUNTERS ("subject to the attached Counter Offer(s)") / REJECTS**; seller signatures; **Acknowledgement of Receipt — signed by an agent with date and time = the Binding Agreement Date**; "For Information Purposes Only" agent and firm block |

The older **TAR F9 (v01/01/2013, 9 pp)** carries the same content with
different numbering: ¶1 parties; ¶1.B items that remain; ¶2.A appraisal
contingency (same box-1/box-2 logic); ¶2.B closing costs and title; ¶2.C loan
type; ¶3 earnest money; ¶4.A closing date; ¶5.B deed name; **¶18 Special
Stipulations on pp. 6–7**. Detect the form by the footer form number, never
by prose — the RF401's own §4.A refers to the closing-date amendment by name.

## RF651 — Counter Offer (1 p)

Numbered exceptions to the referenced agreement. Each one REPLACES the
matching original term; anything not mentioned stands. The form says prior
COUNTER OFFERS not restated are excluded — that clause is about earlier
counters, not the original agreement. Bottom: the responding party's
ACCEPTED / REJECTED / COUNTERED box, signatures, and an **Acknowledgement of
Receipt with date and time — that is the Binding Agreement Date** when the
counter is what was accepted.

## RF657 — Closing Date / Possession Date Amendment

One page, one new date. Arrives after the fact. If one exists, it overrides
§4.A and the summary says so.

## Builder forms

- **D.R. Horton "NSH Home Purchase Agreement"** — §2 price breakdown table:
  Base Price / Lot Premium / Options / Option Incentive / **Sales Price
  Adjustment** / Total. The Total is the price; the base price at the top is
  not, and has been reported as the price by mistake. A "Special Discount"
  adjustment is a price reduction, not seller-paid assistance — describe it,
  do not put it in the concession amount. §9(d) punch-list terms; §19
  closing date defined off the effective date; §20 PUD/HOA.
- **Ole South "Contract for Sale of Real Estate, New Construction"** —
  price under CONSIDERATION; **concessions paragraph on p. 2**, often
  uncapped ("seller pays the balance of closing costs, points, prepaids and
  title") — leave the amount blank and quote the text; ATTACHMENT A options
  list; "Substantially Complete" definition that permits closing with
  driveway, grading and landscaping unfinished — drives as-is vs
  subject-to-completion and whether a 1004D follows.
- PUD / HOA language on builder forms says "IS / IS NOT" — test the
  negative first; a leading-IS test reads "IS NOT" as yes.

## Traps that produced wrong findings on real files

1. **Text layers lie.** Scrambled ToUnicode maps extract as cipher text; a
   fax scan extracts nothing; one PDF had a clean counter-offer page in
   front of a scrambled agreement. Render every page.
2. **Offer date is not the contract date.** Buyers sign with the offer date
   on the signature page; the binding date is the acknowledgement of
   receipt, often a day or more later. Drafts have carried both.
3. **Counter supersedes.** A draft narrated the original $22,100 credit as if
   the counter had only nudged it; the counter had replaced it with $25,000.
   Read the counter first and name the governing document beside each figure.
4. **Deed references date the seller's purchase.** The RF401 §1 deed
   book/page matched a TPAD executor's deed recorded ten weeks earlier.
   Match it every time.
5. **Loan-figure percentages are not the loan type.** 96.5% looks like FHA;
   the Conventional box was checked and the order was conventional. The box
   and the order govern.
6. **"Listing 2" and other leftovers.** Any party, date or count in a draft's
   contract narrative that matches no page in this folder is another file's.
