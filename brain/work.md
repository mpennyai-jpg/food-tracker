# Work

One-line summary: Pinewood Appraisal, residential appraisal for AMCs and big banks across Middle Tennessee.

## Current role

Owner and sole appraiser, Pinewood Appraisal. Mostly refinances and purchases for AMCs and national banks. Very little private or independent work.

## Clients

- AMCs: Clarity, Class, Service (most revisions, in that order), Trilateral.
- Big national banks pay $475 and would like it cheaper.
- One good local client pays $525 to $575.

## Coverage area

- Preferred: Murfreesboro and Rutherford County.
- Also: Lincoln, Franklin, Davidson, Bedford, Cannon, Coffee, Williamson counties.
- Most work is outside Rutherford County.

## Product mix

Does: 1004, 1073 condo, 1004C manufactured (no objection), 2 to 4 unit (a few), hybrids (about 1 a month), 1004D constantly.
Won't do: reviews.
Turns down: subjects with no comps, one-off properties in the millions, files that will obviously be a fight.

## Skills

Comp selection, market-based adjustments with regression support, bracketing discipline, geographic batching of inspections. Twenty years in the field.
