# Scheduling and drive time

One-line summary: how inspection days and desk days run, and what travel costs.

## Travel

| Trip | Time gone |
|---|---|
| One deal in Rutherford County | ~1 hour |
| Any trip out of the house, minimum | 2 hours |
| Lincoln County | 3 to 4 hours |

Most work is outside Rutherford County. He batches inspections by geography when he can. Example loop: down to Lincoln County (Petersburg, Fayetteville), back through Shelbyville, then Murfreesboro. Gone 7 hours, 5 inspections. The cost is coming home and typing until 2 in the morning.
