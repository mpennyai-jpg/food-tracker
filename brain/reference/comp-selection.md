# Comp selection

One-line summary: Todd's comp hierarchy, age limits, distance rule, and bracketing discipline.

## Hierarchy, subdivision deals

1. Subdivision
2. Distance
3. Everything else

## Hierarchy, one-off or rural deals

Distance does not govern. He is looking for the correct comp. "You never really find the perfect comp."

## Age of sale

Three years back is hard. Even for a strong comp, 18 months, maybe 2 years. Beyond that it would take the perfect comp on a very weird deal. Never to hit a number.

## Distance commentary rule

Any comp beyond roughly 10 miles, where it is not obvious the deal is rural, gets discussed in the report. Example framing: went 20 miles because the comp has an in-ground pool and a workshop matching the subject, then address whether the two areas are similar.

## Bracketing

Rarely lets anything go unbracketed.

- Baths: will let it go if the subject is extreme (4.1 baths against a pool of 4-bath comps is fine).
- Bedrooms: 5 or 6 bedrooms can go unbracketed.
- Bed placement: 1 bedroom on the main and 2 in the basement, he will try hard to bracket.
- Site size: bracket.
- Square footage / GLA: do our best to bracket.

It would take the data simply not existing for him to skip it.
