# Fees, volume, and capacity

One-line summary: the numbers that decide whether any workflow change is worth it.

## Volume

| Pace | Feel |
|---|---|
| ~10/month | Slow month |
| 4/week (~16/month) | Good, sustainable |
| 5/week | Getting close to the edge |
| 6/week with a difficult deal | Underwater |
| 7/week | Would love the money; current workflow cannot carry it |

## Fees

- Standard: $525.
- Frequently $475. Most big national banks pay this and want it cheaper.
- One good local client: $525 to $575.

## Labor per file

6.5 hours average at the computer per file, excluding Laura's time, drive time, and inspection time.

## Effective hourly rate

- Desk only: 6.5 hours at $475 to $525 is roughly $73 to $80 per hour.
- With about 2 hours of drive and inspection: closer to $47 to $52 per hour.
- This number is the argument for or against every workflow change.

## Laura's cost

$30 per file, roughly $480 a month at 16 files. Real ROI unknown; time one file without her.
